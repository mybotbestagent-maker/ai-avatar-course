# WHERE TO FIND YOUR PRINT FILES

All files are in the folder: **brand-book/print/**

---

## 1) Car magnets
Folder: `brand-book/print/magnets/`

| Upload to printer | Size |
|---|---|
| `car-magnet-18x12.pdf` | 18×12 in (best for car door) |
| `car-magnet-24x12.pdf` | 24×12 in (wide) |
| `car-magnet-12x18.pdf` | 12×18 in (vertical) |

---

## 2) Business cards
Folder: `brand-book/print/`

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `business-cards-vistaprint.pdf` | VistaPrint 3.5×2 in (front + back) |
| `business-cards-with-bleed.pdf` | Same with bleed |

## 3) T-shirts (front + back)

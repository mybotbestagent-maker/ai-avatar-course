## Online templates competitors copy (structure)

These are the “industry standard” decks painters use:

1. **Proposify — Painting Proposal**  
   https://www.proposify.com/proposal-templates/painting-proposal-template  
   Sections: Cover → About Us → Testimonials → Job description → Materials & labor → Schedule → Terms & signature

2. **Better Proposals — Painting Proposal** (claims $23M won for painters)  
   https://betterproposals.io/proposal-templates/painting-proposal-template  
   Sections: Cover → What we’ll do → Process → Case study → Pricing table → Next steps → Contract → Pay now

3. **Proposal Kit — Painting contractor sample**  
   https://www.proposalkit.com/pdf/sample-business-proposal/painting_contractor_business_proposal.pdf

## What almost everyone does (boring / same)

- Dense 1-page capability sheet OR dry Word-style estimate
- Logo + blue/gray corporate colors
- Bullet lists: competencies, differentiators, NAICS codes
- Little storytelling, few photos of *process*
- Weak “what happens on paint day” narrative
- Almost nobody shows **before/after AI preview** or bilingual PM as a hero feature

## What Gold Hands can do that they don’t (unique angles)

1. **AI house preview** — show the client their home painted before signing  
2. **Miami-specific** — sun, salt, HOA, elastomeric, Coral Gables rules  
3. **EN / ES / RU PM** — rare in competitor decks  
4. **5-year transferable warranty** — put it on its own slide visually  
5. **Photo-led story** — hero job photos, not just logo + text walls  
6. **Job-site identity** — flags / lawn signs / crew look as part of the brand story  
7. **Clear 5-step process** with icons and a day-by-day timeline (not a paragraph)

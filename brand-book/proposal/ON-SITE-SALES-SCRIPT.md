# Gold Hands Painting — On-Site Sales Script
## For today’s client visit (bring phone + this proposal PDF)

**Goal of the visit:** Trust → clear process → written next step → deposit / book date.

---

### 0) Before you ring the doorbell (30 seconds)
- Open the proposal PDF on your phone/iPad
- Have calculator / tape measure ready
- Smile, introduce yourself with full name + company

**Say:**
> “Hi, I’m ______ with **Gold Hands Painting**. Thanks for having me — I’ll walk the job with you, explain exactly how we work, and leave you a clear written estimate. No pressure.”

---

### 1) Opening — who we are (1–2 min)
Show **page 1–2** (team + work photos).

**Say:**
> “We’re a local Miami crew — Miami-Dade and Broward. Same Gold Hands team people already trust for home service. For painting we focus on clean prep, clear price, and a finish that holds up in Florida sun and humidity.”

**Ask:**
> “Quickly — is this mainly **interior**, **exterior**, or **both**? And is there a deadline — guests, listing, HOA, move-in?”

*Listen. Write it on page 7 worksheet.*

---

### 2) Discovery — what they care about (3–5 min)
Walk room by room / facade.

**Ask (pick what fits):**
> “What’s bothering you most right now — color, peeling, stains, builders’ texture, or just a refresh?”
>
> “Anyone living here during the work — kids, pets, work-from-home?”
>
> “HOA or condo board — do we need a COI or color approval?”
>
> “Have you had painters here before? What went wrong or right?”

**Bridge:**
> “Got it. I’ll show you exactly how we protect the home and how payment works so there are no surprises.”

---

### 3) Process — how we work (2–3 min)
Show **page 3** (6 steps like your friend’s deck).

**Say (point to each photo):**
> “1 — We cover furniture and floors.  
> 2 — We repair cracks and holes.  
> 3 — We tape trim for sharp lines.  
> 4 — Prime as needed, then cut in and roll.  
> 5 — We clean every day.  
> 6 — We inspect **with you** before final payment.”

**Ask:**
> “Does that match what you expected — or is there something you want us to be extra careful about?”

---

### 4) Money flow — assessment → deposit → work → final (2 min)
Show **page 4**. This is critical.

**Say:**
> “Here’s the money side, very simple:  
> **Assessment** is free.  
> You get a **written quote**.  
> To lock the date we take a **deposit** — usually about 30–50%, written on the quote.  
> We do the work.  
> We walk through together.  
> You pay the **balance when you’re happy**.”

**Say:**
> “No surprise add-ons. If something extra comes up, we write a change order and you approve it first.”

**Ask:**
> “Does that payment structure work for you?”

---

### 5) Guarantees — remove fear (1–2 min)
Show **page 6**.

**Say:**
> “We’re insured and background-checked. On premium 2-coat work you get our **5-year transferable warranty path** — peeling, blistering, flaking, excessive fading. And if workmanship isn’t right, we come back and make it right.”

**Optional Miami line:**
> “For exterior we also talk elastomeric options — Miami sun and salt are hard on cheap paint.”

---

### 6) Soft close — book the next step (2 min)
Show **page 7–8**. Fill what you can on-site.

**Say:**
> “I’ll finalize the written numbers after I measure everything today. Typical start window is **48–72 hours** once the deposit is in — sometimes same week.”

**Trial close:**
> “If the number lands in a range that works, are you looking to **start this week or next**?”

**If yes:**
> “Perfect — I’ll send the written estimate today/tonight. Once you approve and the deposit clears, we lock your crew and start date.”

**If “I need to think / talk to spouse”:**
> “Totally fair. I’ll still send the written quote so you have everything in one place — process, warranty, and price. Can I follow up tomorrow by text?”

---

### 7) Objection cheat-sheet

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| “Too expensive” | “I hear you. The quote is itemized — prep is where cheap jobs fail. Want me to show a good / better option with different paint systems?” |
| “I’ll get 3 quotes” | “Smart. When you compare, check what’s included: repairs, coats, cleanup, warranty, and who actually shows up. I’ll make ours easy to compare.” |
| “Can you do it cheaper cash?” | “We can discuss scope — fewer rooms or standard materials — but we don’t cut prep. That’s what protects your home.” |
| “I’m not ready” | “No problem. I’ll leave the proposal. When you’re ready, same number — (786) 788-8714.” |

---

### 8) Closing line (always)
> “Thanks for your time. You’ll have a clear written estimate from Gold Hands Painting — and when you’re ready, we’ll take care of the house like it’s our own. Here’s my number: **(786) 788-8714**.”

02 · Logo

Primary mark

The approved logo is the gold metallic painter badge with paint splash energy. Use this exact artwork — do not redraw, recolor, or add a phone number into the mark.

Clear space

Keep empty space around the logo equal to at least the height of the “PAINTING” bar. Never crowd with other graphics or crop the paint splashes.

Minimum size

Print: 1.0 in wide minimum. Digital: 120 px wide minimum. For favicons use the full badge cropped square from exports.

Do

  • Use official PNG/PDF files from this pack
  • Place on navy, white, or paper backgrounds
  • Keep proportions locked

Don’t

  • Stretch, outline, or add drop shadows
  • Put phone number inside the logo art
  • Recolor gold/navy/orange elements

Print note

The logo’s metallic gold is a raster effect. For offset/VistaPrint, print from the provided PNG/PDF as CMYK conversion; do not expect true foil unless you order foil separately. Orange + navy solid colors should be matched as closely as possible to HEX above.

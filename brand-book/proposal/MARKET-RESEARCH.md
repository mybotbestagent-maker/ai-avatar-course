# Gold Hands Painting — Market Research (Aug 2026)

Sources: Sherwin-Williams PRO+ / Project Bids, Behr PRO estimating guidance, PPG color-sales training, FacadeColorizer contractor close-rate data, Miami-Dade/Broward cost & hiring guides 2026, Kompozit USA product positioning, IK Home Pros / Giraffe Painters sales materials (user references).

## 1. Who is the client (ICP)

**Primary:** Owner-occupied single-family / townhome in Miami-Dade & Broward  
- Interior refresh, exterior stucco fade/peel, or both before guests / listing / HOA notice  
- Speaks English and/or Russian (our materials); often compares 2–3 bids  
- Fears: messy crew, wrong color, peeling in humidity, ghosting after deposit  

**Secondary:** Condo / HOA boards  
- Needs COI, color approval, clean common-area process  
- Buys on insurance + documentation + schedule reliability  

**Tertiary:** Pre-sale / investor flips  
- Wants speed + neutral colors + clear written price  

**What they actually buy (not “paint”):**  
1. Confidence in color (PPG: color is the #1 homeowner anxiety)  
2. A finish that survives Florida UV / humidity / mildew  
3. A crew that protects the home and finishes on time  
4. A written scope they can compare to other bids

## 3. Miami / South Florida market facts

- Climate kills cheap paint: humidity, UV, salt air, mildew → prep + climate-rated systems win  
- Interior typical: ~$2.5k–$7k+; exterior often $5k–$18k depending on prep/size  
- Fresh paint = high perceived ROI for listing / showing  
- FL deposit culture: homeowners wary of high upfront; prefer milestones + walkthrough before final  
- Our locked terms (30–50% / 30-30-20) must be framed as **schedule + materials booking**, with final tied to acceptance

## 4. Products / packages to sell

**Jobs (what we sell):**  
1. Interior walls & ceilings refresh  
2. Exterior Florida system (stucco / body + trim)  
3. Full home (interior + exterior)  
4. Condo / HOA (COI-ready)  

**Material tiers (Good / Better / Best) — industry standard:**  

| Tier | Position | System (our offer) | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Standard (Good)** | Value / promo | Kompozit Standard · qualifies for **$0.99/sq ft** when painted area > 3,000 | Large walls, budget refresh |
| **Pro (Better)** ★ sweet spot | Performance | Kompozit Pro / climate systems · 2-coat · fuller prep | Most Miami homes |
| **Premium (Best)** | Max durability / look | Top Kompozit / SW Duration-class option · full prep + extras (doors/trim accents) | Exterior, HOA, listing |

Each tier must differ by **visible scope** (prep depth, coats, surfaces), not just price.

## 5. Winning presentation structure (research → pages)

**Max 3 pages. Sell why us + packages + next step.**

1. **Client + climate problem + trust + AI color confidence**  
2. **Products: 3 tiers + what FREE is worth + $0.99 offer**  
3. **Process (why each step) + money milestones + warranty + CTA**  

Must include: named systems, prep list, visual preview, insurance/COI, warranty, free visit/touch-ups, phone CTA.

## 6. Messaging rules from research

- Sell **risk removal**, not feature lists  
- Shift conversation from “cheapest?” → “which package?”  
- Specify brand/line/coats  
- Show **why free visit** and **why free touch-ups** (Giraffe/IK)  
- Florida-first: humidity / mildew / UV (Kompozit story)  
- No stretched 5-page process dump

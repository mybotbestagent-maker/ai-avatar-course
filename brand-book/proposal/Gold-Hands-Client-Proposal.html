1–2 · Assessment & quote

We walk every surface, note repairs, discuss colors, and send a written itemized estimate. Valid usually 30 days. Most estimates schedule within 24–48 hours.

3 · Prepayment (deposit)

A deposit reserves your start date and covers material ordering. Typical structure for residential jobs:

~30–50% deposit to start · balance on completion

Exact split is written on your quote for this project.

4 · Work days

Your PM confirms arrival window. We protect the home, complete agreed scope, and clean daily so you can still live in the house. Typical interior: 2–3 days. Exterior: 3–5 days (weather windows).

5–6 · Completion & final payment

We do a room-by-room walkthrough with you. Touch-ups fixed before final invoice. Balance due when the job is accepted. Warranty paperwork provided on premium 2-coat work.

Payments we accept

Cash · Card · Zelle · Venmo · Apple Pay · booking deposit when required. Receipts provided. Change orders only in writing — never a surprise add-on.
